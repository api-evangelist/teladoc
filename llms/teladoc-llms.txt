# Teladoc Health

> Teladoc Health is a global virtual-care company (telehealth, behavioral health, chronic-condition management and expert medical opinion). Its developer surface is the Solo platform: a partner-gated REST API (the Solo External API, served under /qapi/v1 on visitnow.org) plus native iOS/Android Mobile SDKs for embedding virtual care into partner apps. Access is granted under a partner agreement and Business Associate Agreement (BAA); authentication is a static Api-Key header.

## APIs
- [Solo External API](https://intouchhealth.github.io/solo-slate/): REST API for patients, appointments, waiting rooms, visit notes, appointment slots, attachments, episodes of care, encounter reports, integrations (Perfect Answer), virtual nursing, and webhooks. Base host: demo.visitnow.org (demo). Auth: Api-Key header.

## Docs
- [Developer Hub](https://api-documentation.teladochealth.com/): Teladoc Health developer documentation portal (partner access).

## Events
- [Webhooks](asyncapi/teladoc-webhooks.yml): 13 event types across patient, appointment, episode and intake lifecycles; payloads AES-256-CBC encrypted. Register at POST /qapi/v1/webhooks.
